# Approved v1.6 spatial diversity and cadence revision

This is the active product authority. Preserve the five-stage, death-only finite-wave game and all unchanged v1.5 systems. The current user-approved revision supersedes prior spatial selection, lightning relay length, late timing and generic flame volley rules.

Pattern identity remains authored, while legal positions, axes, source sides and bounded transformations vary. Measure raw candidates separately from fairness-accepted waves and simulation-played events. Avoid unexplained omitted rows, columns or tiles and excessive consecutive reuse outside intentional motifs. Do not require exact statistical uniformity: geometry and reachable paths legitimately filter candidates.

The single-tile lightning relay is a finite long series of exactly one targeted cell per strike, with no simultaneous pending or active strike in that motif. Each warning starts shortly after the previous damage ends and locks its target. Other lightning motifs retain local clusters and horizontal/vertical short segments. All strikes in one wave use one warning lead and one active duration; no electric field exists.

Lightning, full-lane beast-head flame and local ground fire keep relaxed openings but approach an ordinary late warning target around 0.6 seconds. Dense spatial restrictions may receive an explicit longer warning. Active occupancy also tapers independently, remains visibly damaging throughout its declared interval and is checked on late entry. The dedicated flame relay retains exactly 0.5-second warning-start spacing.

Generic five-line flame volleys are five distinct complete rows or five distinct complete columns, with any of six omitted lanes. Four-line volleys favor a single orientation over mixed crossing. One-, two- and three-line variants remain available with decreasing isolated-line weight. Parallel selections may be adjacent or separated. Interwoven pocket and other authored flame patterns retain their own geometry.

Directional ground-fire groups may progress multiple horizontal and vertical paths together. The next group may warn only after the last cell of every current path ignites; residual burning may overlap groups. Only chain and directional progressions are cellwise. Islands and fragments warn/ignite whole batches together.

Mixed compositions must produce visible lightning with useful frequency in accepted and played waves. Bounded diversity selection may prioritize lightning when recent accepted waves lack it, but every candidate and fallback still requires complete collision/disclosure validation and witness replay. Never mutate announced attacks. Preserve finite waves and one point per survived wave.

Acceptance requires a separate `docs/evidence/v16/` directory containing candidate, accepted and played distribution/timing measurements, rejection/fallback counts, deterministic tests, production-browser/offline results and inspected phone frames. Automation does not certify human randomness, balance, fun or device feel.
