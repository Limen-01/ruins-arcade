# Approved v1.5 warning-disclosure and pattern revision

This is the active product specification. Preserve the five-stage finite-wave game, scoring, controls, art, accessibility, persistence and PWA. It supersedes v1.4 arrow-speed, electric-field, flame-catalog and ground-fire progression rules.

Whole-wave swept-collision reachability remains necessary. Fairness must also consider what warnings have been disclosed when the player first commits to a move. A route that works only with foreknowledge of hidden targets is not sufficient. Test viable early directions against later revelations, reject hidden traps and keep a real correction opportunity. The bounded disclosure check examines the first hidden follow-up in the first 1.2 seconds. A direction counts as a reasonable commitment only if the player can survive the already visible attacks while holding that direction until the follow-up and the declared 0.25-second reaction allowance. Each such commitment must also have a complete-wave escape. The 900-node disclosure search and full 2400-node swept-collision search replay accepted witnesses. This is a practical first-commitment safeguard, not a universal proof of human fairness for every later reveal or every human choice.

One exterior source warning corresponds to one arrow projectile. Ordinary arrow waves freeze a single travel speed for all their batches; the authored `arrow-slow-fast` motif alone alternates speed classes. Reusing a source requires distinguishable warning cycles. Sequential warning intervals belong to patterns, not one global 0.2-second rule.

All lightning remains a visible descending strike. A wave freezes one short or longer strike duration and a consistent warning/activation cadence. No charged floor field. Preserve clustered and short-line motifs with varied feasible placements. Add a rapid random single-tile relay whose next warning starts after the previous strike ends; targets lock at warning.

The dedicated beast-head flame catalog has five entries: multi-line simultaneous, alternating bands, moving corridor, interwoven safe pocket and sequential relay. Remove horizontal/vertical seal. Raise selection of multi-line and pocket. Sequential relay warning starts are exactly 0.5 seconds apart, independent of pressure. Alternating bands and moving corridor use their own 1.0-second warning-start rhythm to preserve a correction window. Shorten only the relay's harmless post-damage recovery by 0.12 seconds, with a 0.16-second floor; its 0.55-second damaging burn remains unchanged. Full six-cell lanes and continuous burn remain.

Only ground-fire chain and directional progressive ignition progress cell by cell. Fire islands and fragmented batches announce and ignite all of their cells together. Chain coverage is smaller than v1.4. Progressive ignition uses finite groups with multiple paths: the next group may warn only after every path's last scheduled cell has ignited, while prior fires may remain active. All such occupancy participates in whole-wave fairness. Complete wave boundaries still require all damage to end and recovery to pass.

Mixed compositions inherit every per-family rule, stay finite and readable, and are subject to full-wave and disclosure-aware validation. Record actual timings, pattern frequencies, rejection/fallback rates and inspected phone-size timed frames. Human and physical-device acceptance remain pending.
